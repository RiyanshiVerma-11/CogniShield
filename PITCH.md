>

## Slide 3: The Mechanism — Four Beats, One Intervention

*Four clean horizontal panels, each with an actual interface screenshot and zero architecture diagrams:*

```
[ Beat 1: SPOT ]    ───>    [ Beat 2: ASK ]    ───>    [ Beat 3: WAIT ]    ───>    [ Beat 4: FLIP ]
Manipulative Cues          One Socratic            12-Second Enforced          Alternative Sourced
Highlighted in DOM         Dilemma Question        Friction Countdown          Search Suggestions
```

### 1. Spot (0ms, On-Device)
- Reads on-screen subtitles and metadata right in the browser.
- Pinpoints high-arousal rhetoric (outrage verbs, false certainty, tribal us-vs-them).
- Displays subtle ambient temperature indicator (Green / Amber / Red).

### 2. Ask (Immediate Cognitive Hook)
- Surfaces one pointed Socratic question from a calibrated local bank:  
  *“What concrete evidence remains if all emotional adjectives are removed?”*

### 3. Wait (The 12-Second Enforced Pause)
- Based on Kahneman's Dual-Process threshold for analytical System 2 activation.
- The action button is physically disabled with a live countdown: `Pause 12s to Reflect...`
- The user is never trapped: they can scroll away or read the original video at any time, but amplification tools require deliberation.

### 4. Flip (The Other Side)
- After 12 seconds, the button unlocks: `See Other Perspectives (3) ➔`
- Replaces algorithmic rabbit holes with counter-perspective search queries across empirical, statutory, and economic viewpoints.

## Slide 4: Who Is Harmed — Named Community & The Second Seat

### Primary Beneficiary: College Student Cohorts
- Not an abstract "all internet users."
- Specifically college students who forward unverified claims, emotional outrage, and academic shortcut summaries in class WhatsApp groups, Reddit, X, and YouTube.
- The harm if we do nothing: peer hysteria, academic integrity violations, and echo-chamber polarization during critical learning years.

### The Second Seat: Faculty Coordinator / Media Literacy Club
- Like ReViva's clinician seat, CogniShield has a secondary stakeholder: the college media literacy coordinator or student club lead.
- **Privacy Boundary:** Faculty never see individual browsing history, student identities, or psychological profiles.
- **What they see:** An anonymous aggregate count:  
  `[PILOT_N] students participated · [FORWARDS_PAUSED] impulsive reactions paused this week.`

### What We Measure
- **12 Seconds of Enforced Friction:** The time delay required before unlocking counter-perspectives or share prompts.

  - Measured Interventions: `[FORWARDS_PAUSED]` pauses completed vs abandoned.

### What We Explicitly Did NOT Measure (Honesty Boundary)
- We do **not** claim to measure permanent IQ increases or clinical cognitive improvements.
- We do **not** claim a fake "95.5% carbon reduction" against fictitious A100 GPU baselines.
- We do **not** claim an "empirically proven universal agency metric"; our Habit Score is an intentional design choice combining dwell friction, quiz completion, and viewpoint variety.

## Slide 7: 90-Second Live Demo Script

*A fail-safe live stage sequence that cannot crash even if venue Wi-Fi dies completely:*

### 0:00 – 0:25: The Outrage Clip (Stage Pack Video 1)
- **Input:** YouTube video — *"AI in Higher Education: Students Turning into Passive Sheep"*
- **Live Behavior:** Ambient HUD glows red (Heat 7.8/10). Highlights emotional phrases: *"destroying education"*, *"cheating epidemic"*.
- **The Intervention:** User clicks CogniShield. 
  - Socratic Question appears: *“Is the real issue the AI tool itself, or testing methods that rely on memorizing information?”*
  - Action button displays: `Pause 12s to Reflect...` with real live countdown.

### 0:25 – 0:50: The Flip & Counter-Perspective
- **Live Behavior:** At 0 seconds, button turns green: `See Other Perspectives (3) ➔`.
- User clicks "Flip the Feed":
  - Algorithmic related videos are replaced by 3 balanced search queries:
    1. OECD Future of Work: Empirical knowledge labor census.
    2. Stanford HAI: Socratic AI interlocutors vs passive summarization.
    3. Harvard EdTech: Evaluating reasoning over static essays.
  - User clicks `Explore ↗`: opens YouTube search with zero censorship of the original.

### 0:50 – 1:15: The Negative Control (The Calm Article That Must NOT Light Up)
- **Input:** Calm economic report from BBC or Le Monde — *"Central bank holds rates steady following quarterly review."*
- **Live Behavior:** CogniShield remains calm green (Score 0.0). Zero false-positive highlight spans.
- **Judge Takeaway:** Proves the system doesn't cry wolf on ordinary sober journalism.

### 1:15 – 1:30: The Second Seat & Pilot Export
- User opens dashboard / popup and clicks `Export Local Session Log`.
- Plain-text record downloads instantly with honest log token `CS-LOG-...` and aggregate pilot placeholders.
- Closing line: *"CogniShield doesn't fight the algorithm. It upgrades the human."*
