Field Guide to Online Manipulation

The 4 most common traps algorithms amplify for viral clicks.

1. False Dilemma (Bifurcation)

Presenting two extreme options as the only choices (e.g. "Either total ban or total destruction") while hiding the nuanced middle ground.

2. Epistemic Confirmation Cascades

Algorithms repeatedly showing variations of your existing view until counter-arguments feel unthinkable or malicious.

3. Affective Outrage Amplification

Using hyper-charged emotional adjectives to trigger immediate anger before logical evaluation can occur.

4. False Consensus & Cherry-Picking

Citing an isolated outlier study as universal proof while ignoring hundreds of contradictory peer reviews.
