4 Common Tricks Used Online

The tricks websites and social media use most to get clicks.

1. Either / Or Trap (Only Two Choices)

Presenting two extremes as the only choices while hiding the sensible middle ground.

2. Echo Chamber Effect

Repeatedly hearing only what you already agree with until other opinions feel unthinkable.

3. Rage Bait (Anger Hooks)

Using emotional words to make you angry before you have time to check if it's true.

4. Cherry-Picking One Example

Using one rare story or study as proof while ignoring everything else.
